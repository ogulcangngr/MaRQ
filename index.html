Surface analysis
Voxel size (0 = automatic)
Grid cell size for downsampling; 0 picks it from the point density.
Field band (× median absolute deviation)
How tightly the flat field plane is fitted. Lower is a stricter field selection. Levelling and orientation are referenced to this field.
Crop margin along the channel
Extra distance beyond the detected channel length at each end of the crop box, in point cloud units.
Crop margin across the channel
Extra distance beyond the detected channel width on each side of the crop box, in point cloud units.
Opening level (fraction of depth) 0.10
Depth fraction where the opening width and length are read. 0.10 is the top 10 % of the depth, close to where the channel meets the flat surface.
Floor level (fraction of depth) 0.90
Depth fraction for the floor width, the channel bottom where the nominal width is defined. 0.90 is the bottom 10 % of the depth.
Floor roughness strip width (fraction) 0.50
Width of the floor roughness strip as a fraction of the channel width (its central part).
Floor roughness strip length
Length of the floor roughness strip along the channel, centred on the channel.
Field roughness strip width
Fixed width of the left and right field strips. Their position is found automatically, just past the raised channel lip, on clean flat field.
Field roughness strip length
Fixed length of the field strips along the channel, centred on the channel.
Advanced
Edge margin
Distance from each edge of the scan discarded before the analysis, removing boundary artefacts.
Display cell size (0 = automatic)
Side of the square cells of the height maps and the 3D view, in point cloud units. Display only: the geometry and the roughness are computed from the points. 0 chooses the finest cell (never below the voxel size) that keeps each map within 160 000 cells.
Display smoothing (cells)
Gaussian blur radius on the display grids, in cells; 0 switches smoothing off.
Depth profile slices
Number of slices along the channel for the local depth and its spread (the widths and the length are read on grid lines).
Header rows to skip
Number of header rows to skip when reading the point cloud file.
Comparison
Grid spacing (µm)
Cell size of the common grid. RQd is defined at a stated cell size; keep the same value for every comparison so the correlations stay comparable.
Smoothing before the slopes (µm)
Gaussian blur before the slopes are computed. At least one grid cell is always applied, for stable slopes.

Number of header rows to skip when reading the point cloud files.

Using MaRQ

Workflow
1. Open a point cloud and Analyse it. 2. Keep as A (the reference) and Keep as B (the surface compared with it). 3. With both filled, Compare. The views of each result are listed in the Browser; the numbers are under Properties.

About MaRQ
MaRQ
Microchannel and Replication Quality Analyzer, version 1.0

MaRQ measures microchannels in optical-profilometry point clouds and compares two surfaces of a channel, A and B, with the height correlation RQ, the directional slope correlations RQdx and RQdy, and RQd, the slope-magnitude correlation over the channel interior.

Please cite

The metrics
RQ: Pearson correlation of the two height maps over the channel-interior mask. RQdx, RQdy: correlations of the signed slopes along and across the channel. RQd: correlation of the slope-magnitude maps at the stated grid cell size (5 µm in the paper); it is the grading metric because it responds to sidewall shape where height correlation saturates.
